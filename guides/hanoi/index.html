What to eat and drink, and where

Prices are per plate, bowl or cup. Each dish lists the places to try it; tap a name for its Google Maps reviews and photos. Fish sauce is in almost everything savoury, so use the phrases below if you're vegetarian.

Ordering vegetarian

Tôi ăn chay
I'm vegetarian (say "toy an chai")
Không thịt, không cá
No meat, no fish
Không nước mắm
No fish sauce
Không trứng
No egg
Quán chay
Vegetarian eatery; look for the word "chay" on signs, often run near pagodas

Getting around Hanoi

Grab and Xanh SM (the green electric taxis) show the price before you book, so there's no haggling.

Rupee converter
