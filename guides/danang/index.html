You're arriving in the wettest weeks of the year. Late October to mid-December is central Vietnam's rainy season: expect rain most days, seas usually too rough to swim, a chance of typhoons, and Hoi An's old town can flood ankle-deep. Da Nang itself keeps running when it floods, so it's the right base. Check the forecast each morning and use the rain switch on the map to see what still works.
